Sequential sections, each with its own colours: a countdown hands over to the next section by itself at zero, a timer counts up until you move on.

Pieces and setlists

Tap a piece to edit its sections. ▶ next to a piece plays it alone: it stops at 00:00. ▶ Start on a setlist plays it in order: each piece opens on its title, and when one ends the title of the next appears.

Timers

+ Add timer adds a section of open length: it counts up from 0:00 until you move on. Hold the right pedal for 2 seconds, or tap Next ›, to go to the next section; a short press pauses and resumes.

Starting from a point

On the title, the coloured bar works like the progress bar of an audio player: tap or drag the head, or use − and +, to start from any point. Reset brings you back to the same point.

Bluetooth pedal

Pair a page-turner pedal in the Bluetooth settings of Android, as a keyboard. On a title, right starts the piece. On a countdown, right pauses and resumes, left resets to the title. On a timer, a short press of right pauses and resumes, holding it for 2 seconds moves on. On a title, left does nothing, so a stray press never changes the piece.

Export and import

Export at the top saves all the setlists in one file; Export inside a setlist saves that one only. Import adds the setlists of a file to those already here, without replacing them. Use it to keep a copy, to move to another phone, or to load the setlists into the web version for ensembles. Export before uninstalling the app: its setlists are lost with it.
